Metrics

Flicker: frame-to-frame change inside the edited region ÷ change in the ring around it. Close to the original video = natural; much higher = flickering; much lower = frozen. Seam: edge strength at the mask border ÷ nearby (≈1 = no visible halo). LaMa calls: how many times the inpainting network runs — the main compute cost.
